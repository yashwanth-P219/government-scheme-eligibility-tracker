How the Smart Tracker Operates

A seamless digital gateway connecting eligible citizens with departmental welfare programs.

1️⃣

Register & Complete Profile

Citizens maintain demographic parameters: age, income, caste category, occupation, and educational status.

2️⃣

Automated Policy Evaluation

Our automated assessment engine cross-verifies demographic parameters against scheme guidelines for accurate qualification.

3️⃣

Apply & Officer Review

Submit applications with instant validation. Designated departmental officers review dossiers and eligibility audit trails.

4️⃣

Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)

Upon administrative sanction, welfare subsidies and scholarship funds are directly provisioned to beneficiary accounts.
